Privacy Policy

Soulemon Radio uploader · Effective 8 November 2026

The Soulemon Radio uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the Soulemon Radio YouTube channel. It is used only with that owner's own Google account. This policy explains what the app accesses and how that information is handled.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos and Shorts to the owner's channel, and set their titles, descriptions, tags, schedules and AI-content labels;
  • set video thumbnails and add videos to the channel's playlists;
  • read the channel's YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, subscribers and, once eligible, estimated revenue).

The app does not access Gmail, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How the information is used

Access is used only to publish Soulemon Radio's own videos and to measure how they perform. The app does not show ads, does not sell or rent data, and does not share data with anyone. Data is not used to train AI models.

Where it is stored

The Google sign-in token is stored on the owner's own computer or in the owner's private Google Cloud project (Secret Manager). Analytics figures are kept in the owner's private spreadsheet. No other copies are made.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions or through the security settings page. Revoking access stops the app immediately. The owner deletes stored tokens and analytics copies on request, within 30 days.
